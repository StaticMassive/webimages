Software

Desktop tools for video and sound, built by Static Massive for creators and pilots. Four apps for Mac and Windows: put a video wall on your Stream Deck, record anything your computer plays, save and edit a video in one place, and pull the static out of analog FPV footage.

Free beta

For Stream Deck

PanoDek

One video. Every key. One click.

Turns any video, GIF or photo into a moving wall across your Stream Deck. Drop a clip on the deck, pick how it splits, and send the whole layout over in one click.

  • Mac
  • Windows beta
  • Stream Deck +, + XL, XL, MK.2, Mini, Neo

Free beta

Audio recorder + pads

AuxRip

Record anything your computer plays.

One click records whatever your computer is playing. Then trim it, mix it, play it back as a pad soundboard, and drag it into OBS, Discord or your editor.

  • Mac
  • Windows
  • WAV + MP3
  • Offline

Free

Video downloader + editor

Toob Stash

Save it. Cut it. Post it.

Saves videos from the sites you already watch and lets you edit them on the spot. Trim, stack, add titles and effects, and export one finished video.

  • Mac
  • MP4, MOV, M4A, MP3
  • No account
