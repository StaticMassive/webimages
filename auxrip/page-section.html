What's inside

A recorder that stays out of the way

Computer audio

Records the sound your computer is playing. This is the default, so there is nothing to set up first.

Microphone, or both

Record a microphone on its own, or with computer audio. Both at once gives you two separate, time-aligned tracks.

48 kHz, 32-bit float

Every recording is 48 kHz stereo, 32-bit float.

Stays out of the way

AuxRip runs in the background: a menu bar icon on Mac, the system tray on Windows, and an optional floating control strip that stays on top.

Shortcuts from any app

Record, pause, resume, stop, start a new track and show or hide the floating controls without switching windows. You can change every shortcut.

Pause, add, split, import

Pause and resume on the same track. Add tracks, record more onto an existing track, split a track, or import audio files.

Trim without deleting

Drag the handles on the waveform to trim. Trimming is non-destructive, so you can pull a handle back at any time.

Offline and private

AuxRip runs offline. Nothing you record leaves your computer. No account, no payment and no telemetry.

Questions

Before you download

Is AuxRip free?

Yes. AuxRip is free software from Static Massive. There is no account, no payment and no telemetry.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with macOS 14.2 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel, or a PC with Windows 10 or 11.

Why does my computer show a security warning?

AuxRip is not registered with Apple or Microsoft, so the first launch shows a warning on both systems. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once, and the install note in the download has the full steps.

Why does my Mac ask for permission to record?

On a Mac, the first recording asks for permission to record system audio. Allow it, then press record again.

What can it record?

The sound your computer is playing, a microphone, or both at once as two separate, time-aligned tracks.

Which formats can I export?

Original (WAV 32-bit float), WAV 24-bit, WAV 16-bit, and MP3 at 320, 192 or 128 kbps. You can choose several at once, and export individual tracks, all tracks layered into one file, or all tracks joined back to back.

Where do my files go?

Exports are saved to your Downloads folder unless you pick another folder.

Does anything leave my computer?

No. AuxRip runs offline and nothing you record leaves your computer.

Am I allowed to record this?

Recording copyrighted audio may not be permitted where you live. Record only audio you have the right to record.
