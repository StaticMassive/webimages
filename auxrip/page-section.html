How it works.

01

Press record

One red button. AuxRip records what your computer is playing, the take grows across the timeline and the meters move. Press it again to stop.

02

Trim and mix

Drag both ends of a clip to trim. Drag a fade in. Move a second clip onto its own track.

03

Drag it out

Hold “Drag out all” and drag the sound out of AuxRip, into OBS, Discord or your editor.

04

Play it as pads

Switch to Pads and every track is a pad. Tap one to play it, hold another, then record straight onto an empty pad.

What's inside

Built for streamers and video makers.

Also for podcasters, people saving meeting audio, and beat makers.

Record in one click

Music, podcasts, meetings, calls, streams. Add your microphone too, or record the mic on its own. Pause and resume. Sound-activated records only while something is playing.

Trim, move and mix

Drag a clip's ends to trim; nothing is ever lost. Fade in and out, set volume and a 7-band EQ per clip, cut with the blade, split at silences. Undo anything.

Drag it straight out

Drag the finished sound into OBS, Discord, an editor or a folder as a file. Or export WAV or MP3: the whole mix, or one file per track.

Every track is a pad

Four banks of 16. Tap to play, hold to play only while held, loop, choke groups. Effects per pad: echo, reverb, pitch, phone, muffle, reverse.

Keys and Stream Deck

Keys 1 to 4, Q to R, A to F and Z to V play pads. Shortcuts work from any app, so a Stream Deck can fire them.

Keep it in time

Optional. Set a tempo, or read it from a music track, and clips snap to bars and beats. Speed pads up or down without changing pitch. Swing.

Time sounds to a video

Drop a video in and it plays above the timeline and pads, in step with them. Extract its audio as a track.

Offline and yours

AuxRip runs fully offline. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts or API keys.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with macOS 14.2 or later, Apple silicon or Intel, or a PC with Windows 10 or 11.

What can it record?

Whatever your computer is playing: a clip from a video, music, a podcast, a meeting or call, a stream, a game. Add your microphone too, or record the mic on its own.

Why does my Mac say it cannot verify the app?

The first time, macOS may say it cannot verify the app. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Installing from Terminal with the line on this page avoids that step.

How do I get the sound into OBS, Discord or my editor?

Drag it straight out of AuxRip and drop it in. Or export WAV or MP3: the whole mix, or one file per track.

Does it work with a Stream Deck?

Yes. AuxRip shortcuts work from any app, so a Stream Deck key can fire a pad.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. AuxRip runs fully offline. Nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts or API keys.
