liner notesabout the film

Kamakaze Tsunami: The Nihon Tapes is a guerrilla whoop film made in Japan. Guerrilla whooping (also spelled guerilla whoop or gorilla whoop) means flying tinywhoop FPV drones through city spots like alleys, stairwells, underpasses and rooftops, low key and unplanned.

This one was flown through Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka. Back alleys, neon side streets, torii gates, 7-Eleven runs and everything that happened between them. It was shot entirely on analog FPV and a handycam and cut like a 90s skate video.

The film is by pilots @kazifpv and @thatbanana, and it's released by Static Massive, an FPV lifestyle brand born out of guerrilla whooping. The bundle is the full film plus a printed zine with photos from the trip and handycam stills pulled off the tape. Cover art by ThatBanana.
