about

an FPV lifestyle brand born out of guerrilla whooping.

transmitting for the people.

It started with analog goggles, a bag of tinywhoops and whatever spot was open that night. Downtown highrises, stairwells, bandos, a lipo swap on the curb. The brand puts out apparel, original FPV photography, zines, apps and films, including Kamakaze Tsunami, a guerrilla whoop film shot on analog and handycam in Japan.

Tinywhoops and analog are home base. 5 inch freestyle, bando and urbex pilots are part of it too. If you've ever threaded a gap you had no business trying, you already get it.

definitionwhat is guerrilla whooping?

Guerrilla whooping is flying tinywhoop FPV drones through city spots like stairwells, parking structures, underpasses and downtown buildings, low key and unplanned.

You'll see it spelled guerrilla whoop, guerilla whoop or gorilla whoop. Same thing. No field, no gates, no club night. A bag of whoops, goggles, a radio, and whatever spot is open.

Bridges, warehouses, wet concrete. Rain doesn't cancel the session, it just makes the clip moodier.

Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka. Back alleys, neon, 7-Eleven runs. The setting for Kamakaze Tsunami.

Any city with a stairwell and a pilot who can't sit still.

questionsquick answers

What is guerrilla whooping?

Flying tinywhoop FPV drones through city spots like stairwells, parking structures, underpasses and downtown buildings, low key and unplanned.

Is gorilla whoop the same as guerrilla whoop?

Yes. Guerrilla whoop, guerilla whoop and gorilla whoop all mean the same style of flying. Guerrilla is the correct spelling.

What drone do you need for guerrilla whooping?

A tinywhoop: a small ducted FPV drone, usually 65 to 75mm on a 1S battery, flown with FPV goggles and a radio.

Is there a guerrilla whoop film?

Kamakaze Tsunami: The Nihon Tapes, shot on analog and handycam in Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka by @kazifpv and @thatbanana. Get the film and zine.

Is guerrilla whooping the same as urbex?

They overlap. Urbex is exploring abandoned or off-limits places. Guerrilla whooping is about flying a tinywhoop through city spots, abandoned or not.

What does Static Massive make?

FPV apparel printed in the US when you order, original FPV photography prints, zines, free apps for pilots, and films.

Is Static Massive the same as Static Mass?

No. Static Massive is an FPV lifestyle brand. Static Mass is an unrelated film journal.
