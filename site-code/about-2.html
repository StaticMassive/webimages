sceneswhere it lives

new york

The mecca. New York put guerrilla whooping on the map: tight blocks, a thousand gaps, and cops who have bigger things to deal with.

los angeles

Downtown parking structures, empty lots, long concrete and skateparks. Still growing, and getting itself on the map.

portland

Bridges, warehouses, wet concrete. One of the fastest growing whoop scenes anywhere. Rain just makes the clip moodier.

japan

Tokyo is massively bigger than New York and the strictest city on this list. Osaka runs more chill and is easier to guerrilla whoop. Kyoto in between. The setting for Kamakaze Tsunami.

europe

Paris, London, Berlin, Madrid, Milan. Old stone, tight streets and stairwells that have been around for centuries. Plenty of lines nobody has flown yet.

south america

São Paulo and Buenos Aires. Big cities, loud streets, pilots who fly like they mean it.

Everywhere else: any city with a stairwell and a pilot who can't sit still.

questionsquick answers

What is guerrilla whooping?

An FPV art form, like skateboarding or graffiti. Pilots fly tinywhoops through the city like a skatepark: grinding handrails, wall-riding skyscrapers, threading gaps in buildings and riding mall escalators. Also spelled guerilla whoop or gorilla whoop.

Is gorilla whoop the same as guerrilla whoop?

Yes. Guerrilla whoop, guerilla whoop and gorilla whoop all mean the same style of flying. Guerrilla is the correct spelling.

What drone is best for guerrilla whooping?

A 65mm tinywhoop on a 1S battery, ideally with 30,000KV or higher motors. It does everything a 75mm does but quieter, which matters in busy spots and strict cities like Tokyo. Fly it with FPV goggles and a pocket-sized radio.

How do you guerrilla whoop without getting kicked out?

Fly at foot level or overhead, never at head level. Scout every angle of the spot first, keep it to one or two packs at busy spots, give people space, and leave when asked, or offer to leave before you are asked.

What should you bring to a guerrilla whoop session?

A 65mm tinywhoop, a pocket-sized radio, FPV goggles worn around your neck, one or two batteries for busy spots, and a light bag or fanny pack for the extras.

Is there a guerrilla whoop film?

Kamakaze Tsunami: The Nihon Tapes, shot on analog and handycam in Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka by @kazifpv and @thatbanana. Get the film and zine.

Is guerrilla whooping the same as urbex?

They overlap. Urbex is exploring abandoned or off-limits places. Guerrilla whooping is about flying a tinywhoop through city spots, abandoned or not.

What does Static Massive make?

Is Static Massive the same as Static Mass?

No. Static Massive is an FPV lifestyle brand. Static Mass is an unrelated film journal.
