Clean · the main feature

Takes the static out.

One scan shows where the static is. You choose how much of the clip to clean, and how hard. It runs on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded.

Restore

Brings back clips that won't play.

Crashed into a tree with the DVR still recording? Pulled the battery before you hit stop? That file won't open. CrashSaver rebuilds it so it plays, and you can save it.

The tour

01

Drop in the clips that won't play

Restore brings back recordings that froze or were cut off: one clip, several, or a whole folder off the card.

02

Repair, watch it back, then save

Repair rebuilds the clip and plays it right here. Nothing is written until you press Save, and your original is never changed.

03

Clean takes the static out

For clips that play but are full of analog static. A quick scan finds the snow, the weak signal and the worst moments.

04

Choose how much to clean

Drag the white line down to clean more of the clip, or up to clean only the worst of it. Quality trades time for a cleaner picture.

05

Compare, then save

Drag across the preview to see before and after, then Clean the whole clip and save it.

Why it took this long

Months of testing before anyone gets it.

CrashSaver is a serious tool with a simple face. It reads every frame, maps the damage across the whole clip, and rebuilds broken picture from the frames around it. Getting that right on real analog footage took months of testing, and it stays in the workshop until it is ready.

Honest limits

It won't invent a flight

It brings back most clips and most of the static. A stretch that is pure static has nothing left to rebuild, so Cut out static skips it.

How much will it cost?

CrashSaver will be a paid app. The price will be announced at launch.

Does it work with DJI, Walksnail or HDZero?

Clean is for analog static. Restore works with MP4 and MOV files from any goggles.

Does it upload my footage?

No. Everything runs on your Mac.

Will it change my original file?

Never. It always writes a new file.

How long does a clean take?

Roughly the length of the clip on Quick, and longer on Best and Max. The app gives you an estimate before you start.

Can it fix a clip that's all static?

No. Nothing is there to bring back, so Cut out static just skips it.

Is there a Windows version?

Windows is planned, but there is no date yet. CrashSaver is a Mac app first, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 11 Big Sur or later.
