Toob Stash.

Save it. Cut it. Post it.

Toob Stash saves videos from the sites you already watch and lets you edit them on the spot. Trim, stack, add titles and effects, and export one finished video. It works great with YouTube and Instagram links. Free for Mac.

How it works

01

Get a video

Copy a video's link and paste it. Choose what to save, press Download, and it lands in your Downloads list.

02

Build your timeline

Drag a saved clip onto a track. Drag your own videos, photos and logos in from the desktop, onto the timeline or straight onto the picture.

03

Trim, cut and move

Drag an edge to trim, click with the blade to cut, drag a clip to move it. Right-click any clip for copy, paste, detach audio and more.

04

Make it look right

Click a picture to move and resize it. Colour, blur, sharpen and vignette on every clip. Titles in any font, typed right on the picture.

05

Export

Mark the in and out points, pick MP4, MOV, M4A or MP3, and press Export. One finished file.

Only in Toob Stash

Trim first. Download only what you keep.

Look up a two-hour stream and it's on your timeline in seconds, before anything downloads. Cut out the minute you want, then press Download. Toob Stash fetches only the parts you kept.

Features

Then make it yours.

Titles on the picture

Press T, double-click the title in the preview and type. Any font, colour, shadow or box.

Effects and transitions

Colour, black and white, blur, sharpen, vignette. Cross dissolve, dip to black or white, push.

Right-click any clip

Copy, paste, duplicate, split, ripple delete, detach audio, change speed.

Export one file

MP4, MOV, M4A or MP3, in 16:9, 9:16, 4:3 or 1:1. Nothing is uploaded.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no account, no watermark.

Which sites does it work with?

It works great with YouTube and Instagram, and with almost any site with public videos: if anyone can watch it without signing in, Toob Stash can usually save it. Sites change often, so Toob Stash keeps its downloader up to date by itself.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. Toob Stash is Mac first, and a Windows version is planned for later.

Where do my videos go?

To a folder you choose, Movies or Downloads to start with. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Can I use my own videos and pictures?

Yes. Drag them in from Finder, or use Import.

Can I use any video I save?

Save and edit videos you have the right to use: your own, ones made to be shared, or ones the owner has said you can use.

macOS says it can't check the app.

Toob Stash is not registered with Apple yet. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open. You only do this once.
